- Over the sample the rate ranged from **6,614** (1999-06-28) to **18,150** (2026-06-08) IDR/USD — a weaker rupiah at the high end.
- Latest (2026-07-31): **18,052** IDR/USD. YTD +7.99%, 1-year +9.43%.
- Sharpest ~quarter of depreciation in the sample: **+37.0%** (2008-08-27 → 2008-11-24, 9,160 → 12,550).
